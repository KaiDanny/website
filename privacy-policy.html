Unless a game-specific notice says otherwise, the five Games use the same categories of platform, storage, and advertising services described in this Policy.

1. Information and Services Used in the Games

The Games are made available through Facebook Instant Games. Depending on how you play and the features you use, information may be processed by the platform or by service providers that support the Games.

1.1 Gameplay information and Facebook Instant Games Cloud Storage

The Games use Facebook Instant Games Cloud Storage to save and retrieve gameplay-related information. Depending on the features available in a particular Game, this may include game progress, completed levels, scores, or game settings and preferences.

This storage is used to support game functionality, such as retaining or restoring gameplay progress. The specific information saved may vary according to the features you use.

1.2 Player profile information

Our Games do not call FBInstant.player.getID() or FBInstant.player.getName() to retrieve a player's Facebook ID or name. This does not mean that Facebook, Meta, or the platform is unable to process information under its own terms when you use its services.

1.3 Analytics

We do not integrate a separate third-party analytics SDK in these Games. The Facebook Instant Games platform and advertising services may nevertheless process service, performance, or advertising-related information as described in their own privacy documentation.

2. Advertising Services

All five Games use Meta Audience Network to provide advertising. In connection with advertising services, Meta and its service providers may process information such as:

  • IP address and general network information;
  • device type, operating system, language, and related technical information;
  • advertising identifiers, where available and permitted by applicable settings and law;
  • ad impressions, clicks, and interactions; and
  • information used for ad delivery, frequency management, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

The information processed and the purposes of processing depend on the applicable Meta services, your device and platform settings, and applicable law. Meta handles information it processes for its own advertising services in accordance with its own policies.

For more information, please review Meta's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with the Games may be used, as applicable, to:

  • provide core gameplay features and maintain game progress;
  • save and restore gameplay-related information through platform storage;
  • display advertising through Meta Audience Network;
  • maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Games; and
  • respond to user questions, requests, or reports.

We do not disclose information to third parties except as needed to operate the Games, provide platform and advertising functionality, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

The Games rely on the following third-party services:

  • Facebook Instant Games / Meta: provides the platform through which the Games are made available and the Cloud Storage functionality used by the Games.
  • Meta Audience Network: provides advertising services for the Games.

These services are subject to the relevant providers' terms and privacy policies. We do not control the independent data practices of Meta. Please review Meta's policies for details about information Meta processes through its platform and advertising services.

Gameplay information saved through Facebook Instant Games Cloud Storage is handled through the platform's storage service. Its availability and retention may be subject to the platform's functionality, settings, and policies.

We retain information that we receive or process only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to address user requests, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet applicable legal requirements.
